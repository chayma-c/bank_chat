### 2.1 VirtuBank Current Account (Compte Courant)

**What it is:**
A everyday checking account for deposits, withdrawals, and transactions.

**What it's good for:**
- Daily spending and bill payments
- Salary deposits
- Standing orders and direct debits
- Checks and mobile payments
- Emergency access to cash

**Key Features:**
- IBAN & international bank details included
- Contactless debit card
- Mobile app for 24/7 management
- No monthly fees if balance > €500
- Monthly fee: €2.99 if balance < €500

**Paperwork & Documents Needed:**
- Valid identity document (passport, national ID, or driver's license)

- KYC questionnaire (name, occupation, source of funds)

**Limits & Rules:**
- Daily withdrawal limit: €2,000
- Daily debit card limit: €5,000
- Standing order limit: No limit
- Monthly statements: Automatic, available in app

## 3. Account Policies & Rules

### 3.1 Card Management

**Card Loss or Theft:**
- Immediately block the card in the mobile app (instant)
- Call support: +33 (0) 800 123 456
- Card replacement issued within 3 business days
- You are not liable for fraudulent charges after blocking
- Replacement card is free; reissue fee: €15

**Forgotten PIN:**
- Reset via mobile app in < 2 minutes
- Request temporary PIN via phone (support line)
- Physical PIN reset at branch (if available)
- No fee for PIN reset

**Card Expiration:**
- Card is automatically renewed 30 days before expiration
- New card arrives 1 week before expiration
- All recurring subscriptions are automatically updated

### 3.2 Password & Security

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include: uppercase, lowercase, number, special character
- Cannot contain your name, birth date, or username
- Password expires every 90 days (reminder sent)
- Previous 5 passwords cannot be reused

**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):**
- Required for: logins from new devices, transfers > €1,000, card blocking
- Methods: SMS OTP, app push notification, biometric
- OTP validity: 5 minutes

**Important:** VirtuBank **never asks for your password or PIN via chat, email, or phone.**

### 3.3 Account Closure

**To Close Your Account:**
1. Ensure balance is €0 or transfer remaining funds to another account
2. Contact support via phone or app
3. Close any linked credit products first
4. Final statement issued within 10 business days
5. Account becomes inactive after 180 days

**Closure Fees:**
- Early closure (within 12 months of opening): €25
- Standard closure: Free
- Balance refunded within 5 business days

### 3.4 Account Inactivity

**Definition:** No transactions (deposits, withdrawals, transfers) for 12 consecutive months.

**What Happens:**
- Month 12: Notification sent via email
- Month 13: Account marked as "inactive" (limited access)
- Month 24: Account eligible for dormancy program
- Year 2+: Monthly €2 inactivity fee applied

**Reactivation:**
- Simply log in or make a deposit
- Inactivity fees waived if account reactivated within 90 days
- After 90 days, fees are permanent

### 3.5 Dispute & Fraud Claims

**Unauthorized Transaction Claim:**
- Report immediately (within 60 days recommended, max 180 days)
- VirtuBank investigates within 10 business days
- Temporary credit issued during investigation
- Resolution within 30 business days
- Full refund if fraudulent activity confirmed

**How to Report:**
- Mobile app > Settings > Report a Problem
- Phone: +33 (0) 800 123 456
- Email: disputes@virtubank.example

**Liability:**
- No liability if reported within 2 business days: €0
- Liability if reported after 2 days but < 60 days: Max €50
- Liability if reported after 60 days: Customer liable for full amount

## 5. Out-of-Scope Topics

The support chatbot **should NOT attempt** to answer:

- **Real account data:** Actual balance, transactions, card details (say: "I don't have access to your account. Check the app.")
- **Real-world banking:** Taxes, legal advice, mortgage qualification (say: "Please consult a financial advisor for that.")
- **Other banks:** Information about competing banks (say: "I can only help with VirtuBank products.")
- **Investments:** Stock tips, cryptocurrency advice, trading (say: "VirtuBank doesn't offer investment services.")
- **Unrelated topics:** Weather, sports, politics, general knowledge (say: "I'm here to help with VirtuBank questions.")

**Fallback Response:**
> "I don't have information about that. Please contact our support team at +33 (0) 800 123 456 or email support@virtubank.example. We're happy to help during business hours (Mon–Fri, 08:00–18:00 CET)."

## 8. Tone & Voice Guidelines for Support Agent

### Do's ✅
- Be professional and reassuring
- Use simple, clear language
- Acknowledge the customer's concern
- Provide specific, actionable steps
- Set realistic expectations
- Follow up with alternatives if you can't help

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't use banking jargon without explaining
- Don't make promises you can't keep
- Don't ask for sensitive data (password, PIN, full card number)
- Don't provide real account information via chat
- Don't invent policies or fees not listed here
- Don't go outside VirtuBank product scope

### Example Interaction

**Customer:** "I can't access my account. What do I do?"

**Support Agent:**
> "I understand that's frustrating. Let's troubleshoot this together.
> 
> First, can you try resetting your password via the login page?
> 1. Go to the VirtuBank login screen
> 2. Click 'Forgot password'
> 3. Enter your email
> 4. You'll receive a reset code via SMS (takes 2 minutes)
> 5. Create a new password (12+ characters, with uppercase, number, symbol)
> 
> If that doesn't work or you don't receive the SMS, please call support at +33 (0) 800 123 456. We're available Mon–Fri, 08:00–18:00 CET."
